The paper "Learning millisecond protein dynamics from what is missing in NMR spectra" describing Dyna-1,
which predicts µs–ms conformational dynamics on motions tied to enzyme catalysis and ligand binding from protein sequences,
has just been accepted by <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10989-4">Nature</a>.

Congratulations to co-first author <a href="/alumni/#Hannah Wayment-Steele">Hannah Wayment-Steele</a> and Gina El Nesr, and everyone else involved, on another great work on protein dynamics!
The project was a collaboration with <a href="https://https://solab.org/">Sergey Ovchinnikov at MIT</a>.
